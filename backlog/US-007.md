# US-007 — Change quantity and remove from the cart

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 3 · **Sprint:** 1 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a buyer, I want to adjust what is in my cart, so that it reflects what I actually
want.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a product is in my cart, when I change its quantity, then the line and the
   cart total both update.
2. Given a product is in my cart, when I remove it, then it is gone from the cart.
3. Given I set a quantity of zero, when it is processed, then the line is removed
   rather than kept at zero.
4. Given my cart is empty, when I look at it, then I see a message saying so and a
   way back to the catalogue.
