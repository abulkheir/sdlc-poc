# US-011 — Edit and delete my own listings

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 3 · **Sprint:** 2 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a seller, I want to change or withdraw what I have listed, so that the catalogue
matches what I can actually supply.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I am signed in as a seller, when I open my listings, then I see all of mine
   and only mine.
2. Given a listing of mine, when I change any of its fields, then the change is
   saved and visible in the catalogue.
3. Given a listing of mine, when I delete it, then it no longer appears in the
   catalogue.
4. Given the same validation rules as creation, when I submit an invalid edit, then
   I am told which field is wrong and nothing is saved.
