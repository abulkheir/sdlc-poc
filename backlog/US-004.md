# US-004 — Register as a buyer or a seller

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 5 · **Sprint:** 1 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a visitor, I want to create an account and say which kind of user I am, so that
the marketplace gives me the right abilities.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I am registering, when I fill in the form, then I must supply an email
   address, a password, and a choice of **buyer** or **seller**.
2. Given I submit an email address that is already registered, when the form is
   processed, then I am told so and no second account is created.
3. Given I submit a password that does not meet the stated rules, when the form is
   processed, then I am told which rule it broke.
4. Given my registration succeeds, when it completes, then I am signed in and my role
   is fixed for the life of the account.
5. Given my account exists, when it is stored, then my password is not recoverable
   from what is stored.
