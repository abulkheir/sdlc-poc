# EPIC-001 — Marketplace storefront

**State:** Active · **Sprint:** 1–2 · **Source:** [[BRD-0.1]]

A shared storefront where visitors browse a catalogue, buyers fill a cart, and
sellers manage their own listings without being able to affect anyone else's.

## Scope

Browsing, search, authentication with two roles, a persistent cart with stock limits,
and seller listing management.

## Out of scope

Checkout, payment, orders, fulfilment, an administrator role, and listing approval.
