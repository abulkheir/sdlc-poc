# US-005 — Sign in and sign out

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 3 · **Sprint:** 1 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a registered user, I want to sign in and out, so that my account and my cart
follow me and nobody else can use them.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I have an account, when I supply the right email and password, then I am
   signed in and the interface reflects my role.
2. Given I supply the wrong email or the wrong password, when I submit, then I am
   refused with a message that does not reveal which of the two was wrong.
3. Given I am signed in, when I sign out, then anything that required a signed-in
   user stops working until I sign in again.
4. Given I am not signed in, when I ask for a page that requires a role, then I am
   sent to sign in rather than shown the page.
