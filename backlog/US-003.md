# US-003 — View a product's detail page

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 2 · **Sprint:** 1 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a visitor, I want to open a product and read about it, so that I can decide
whether I want it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I am looking at the catalogue, when I select a product, then I see its name,
   full description, price, category and whether it is in stock.
2. Given I am on a product's page, when I share or reload the address, then the same
   product is shown.
3. Given I ask for a product that does not exist, when the page loads, then I see a
   "not found" message rather than an error.
