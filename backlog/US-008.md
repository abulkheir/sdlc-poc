# US-008 — Find my cart after signing back in

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 3 · **Sprint:** 2 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a buyer, I want my cart to still be there next time, so that I do not have to
start again on another day or another machine.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I have products in my cart, when I sign out and sign back in, then the cart
   is exactly as I left it.
2. Given I have products in my cart, when I sign in on a different browser, then the
   same cart is there.
3. Given two different buyers are signed in, when each looks at their cart, then
   neither sees anything belonging to the other.
