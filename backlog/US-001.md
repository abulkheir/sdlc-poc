# US-001 — Browse the catalogue

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 3 · **Sprint:** 1 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a visitor, I want to see the products on offer, so that I can tell whether this
marketplace sells anything I want before I bother signing up.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I am not signed in, when I open the storefront, then I see a list of
   products, each showing its name, price and category.
2. Given the catalogue has products, when the list renders, then every product in the
   catalogue appears — nothing is hidden behind a sign-in.
3. Given a product is out of stock, when I see it in the list, then it is marked as
   such and remains visible.
4. Given the catalogue is empty, when I open the storefront, then I see a message
   saying so rather than a blank page.
