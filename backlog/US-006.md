# US-006 — Add a product to the cart

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 3 · **Sprint:** 1 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a buyer, I want to put products in a cart, so that I can gather what I want in
one place.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I am signed in as a buyer, when I add a product, then it appears in my cart
   with a quantity of one.
2. Given a product is already in my cart, when I add it again, then its quantity
   increases rather than a second line appearing.
3. Given I am signed in as a seller, when I look at a product, then no way to add it
   to a cart is offered, and the API refuses if I ask anyway.
4. Given I am not signed in, when I try to add a product, then I am sent to sign in.
