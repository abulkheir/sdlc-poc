# US-010 — Create a listing

**State:** Ready · **Points:** 5 · **Sprint:** 2 · **Parent:** [[EPIC-001]]

## Story

As a seller, I want to put a product up for sale, so that buyers can find it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given I am signed in as a seller, when I create a listing with a name,
   description, price, category and stock count, then it is saved against my account.
2. Given my listing is saved, when a buyer browses the catalogue, then it appears
   there immediately.
3. Given I leave a required field empty or give a price or stock that is not a valid
   positive number, when I submit, then I am told which field is wrong and nothing is
   saved.
4. Given I am signed in as a buyer, when I try to create a listing, then the API
   refuses.
