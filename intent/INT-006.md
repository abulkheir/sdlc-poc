# INT-006 — Add a product to the cart

> Derived from [[US-006]]. The work item is the source of truth; this is the
> originator's account of what they actually meant, in their own words, and it is
> what the spec is written against.

## What I want

Someone browsing the shop finds three or four things they might buy and wants to
keep them together while they carry on looking. Right now there is nowhere to put
them, so the only way to compare is to keep tabs open.

I want a button on a product that puts it aside. Press it again on the same product
and I should have two of that thing — not the same product listed twice, which is
what I have seen on other sites and it always looks broken.

## Who it is for

Buyers. Sellers have no reason to collect products — they are not shopping here,
they are selling. If a seller somehow manages to ask for this, the answer should be
no, and it should be no from the server, not just a hidden button.

## What "better" looks like

I can gather things without losing them, and the count in the corner tells me how
many I have picked up so far. Nothing surprises me later.

## What I do not want yet

Buying anything. There is no checkout, no payment, no order. This is a place to put
things, nothing more.

## What worries me

A seller says they have five of something. If two buyers each put five in their
cart, have I promised ten? I do not think we have decided that, and I do not think
we need to decide it before people can start collecting things — but somebody should
write down that we have not decided it, because eventually it will matter.

## What is out of my hands

Whether the person is signed in. If they are not, send them to sign in first — I do
not want a cart that belongs to a browser and then vanishes.
