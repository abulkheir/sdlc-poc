# INT-004 — Register as a buyer or a seller

> Derived from [[US-004]]. The work item is the source of truth; this is the
> originator's account of what they actually meant.

## What I want

Someone arrives, likes what they see, and wants in. They should be able to make an
account there and then — an email address, a password, and a choice of whether they
are here to buy or to sell.

That choice is not a preference they can change later. It decides what the whole
place looks like to them. Somebody who signs up to sell should never see a cart, and
somebody who signs up to buy should never be asked to list a product.

## Who it is for

Anyone who is not signed in yet. There is no invitation, no waiting list, and
nobody approves them.

## What "better" looks like

The form tells me what is wrong **while I am fixing it**, not after I have submitted
and lost what I typed. If my password is too short, it says which rule I broke — not
"invalid input". And if I have an account already, it says so plainly instead of
quietly failing.

When it works, I am already signed in. Nobody should have to register and then
immediately log in with the details they just typed; that has always felt like the
system forgetting what just happened.

## What I do not want yet

An email arriving to confirm anything. A way to reset a forgotten password. A person
somewhere approving new sellers. All of those are real and all of them are later.

## What worries me

The password. I do not want us storing it in a way that means a copy of the database
is a copy of everyone's password. I am told there is a standard way to do this and
that we should simply do it, and I would rather hear that we have than be reassured.

And I worry about the role being a one-way door. I am choosing that deliberately —
it keeps everything else simple — but if somebody signs up as a buyer and later
wants to sell, today the answer is a second account. Say that out loud when anyone
asks, rather than pretending it is not a limitation.

## What is out of my hands

What counts as a strong enough password. I have an opinion about length and no
opinion about anything else. Whoever owns that decision should make it, write it
down, and I will live with it.
